# Design DNA — Existing 110 Creatives

## Visual Quality Benchmark
These are **photorealistic, agency-grade Meta ad creatives** — NOT illustrations or generic stock photos.
They look like real Facebook/Instagram ads that would run in the wild.

## Layout Patterns by Angle

### Before / After
- **Split-screen**: left half = problem (dark, gloomy, damaged), right half = solution (bright, clean, new)
- "BEFORE" label top-left, "AFTER" label top-right — large bold white text
- Bottom section: dark semi-transparent gradient overlay with bold white headline + subheadline + pill CTA button
- Trust badge (shield icon, "TRUSTED LOCAL EXPERTS", 4.9★, 1,200+ Jobs) in top-left corner on white circle
- CTA button: orange pill shape "GET FREE QUOTE" or green "Book Free Inspection"
- Headline example: "BEFORE & AFTER — SEE THE DIFFERENCE" / "Your Roof Deserves Better"

### Hero Shot  
- Full-bleed photorealistic scene (beautiful interior, job site, product)
- Top section: large bold white headline text directly on photo (with subtle dark gradient at top)
- Center: white pill/rounded CTA button "GET A FREE ESTIMATE"
- Bottom-left: dark circle badge "SERVING YOUR CITY SINCE 2005"
- No heavy overlay — photo is the hero, text floats on top

### Features / Benefits
- Dark navy blue background (not a photo — designed card)
- Bold white headline top-left: "Why 2,400 Homeowners Choose Us"
- 3 checkmark rows: large green checkmark + icon + benefit text (white, readable)
- Right side: 3D rendered product image (HVAC unit, pipe, electrical panel, etc.)
- Bottom: green pill CTA "Get Free Quote"
- Clean, structured, infographic-style

### UGC / Testimonial
- Full-bleed lifestyle photo (happy family, homeowner, customer)
- Top: bold white headline "Real Customers. Real Results."
- Center: white speech bubble with quote text + customer name
- 5 gold stars below the bubble
- Bottom: orange pill CTA "Book Today"
- Warm, authentic feel

### Roofing Before/After (variant)
- Pure split-screen, no bottom text panel — text overlaid directly on photos
- "BEFORE" top-left white bold, "AFTER" top-right white bold
- Bottom gradient overlay with headline + subheadline + green CTA button

## Typography System
- **Headlines**: Extra-bold, white, large (60-80px equivalent), clean sans-serif (Inter/Montserrat style)
- **Subheadlines**: Regular weight, white or light grey, 24-32px
- **CTA buttons**: Bold, pill/rounded shape, orange (#E8621A) or green (#22C55E), white text
- **Labels**: ALL CAPS, bold, white

## Color System
- **HVAC**: Navy blue background (#0F1B2D), green accents (#22C55E), orange CTA (#E8621A)
- **Roofing**: Dark charcoal, green CTA, white text
- **General**: Dark overlays for text legibility, bright accent CTAs

## Key Quality Markers
1. Photorealistic photography (not illustrations)
2. Text is ALWAYS legible — dark scrim or white panel behind text
3. CTA button is always present and prominent
4. Trust element always present (badge, stars, years in business)
5. Clean, uncluttered layout — max 3-4 text elements
6. Professional lighting in photos
7. Aspect ratio: 1:1 square (current) — need to regenerate in 9:16 vertical

## 9:16 Adaptation Rules
- Before/After: stack vertically (BEFORE top half, AFTER bottom half) OR keep side-by-side with taller frame
- Hero Shot: extend top and bottom, headline at top, CTA at bottom
- Features/Benefits: add more vertical space, stack features with more breathing room
- UGC/Testimonial: photo fills top 60%, text/quote fills bottom 40%
- All text must remain in center 80% safe zone
